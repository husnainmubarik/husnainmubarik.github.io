## 🎴 Cardistry

Cardistry is "the performance art of card flourishing" – the art of spinning, tossing, cutting, and manipulating cards in aesthetically pleasing ways. It combines dexterity, creativity, and visual appeal.

This hobby helps develop:
- Fine motor skills and hand-eye coordination
- Patience and attention to detail
- Creative expression through movement

Learn more about cardistry [here](https://playingcarddecks.com/blogs/all-in/what-is-cardistry).
